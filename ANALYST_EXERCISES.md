# Hands-on Analyst Exercises

1. **Evidence monitoring:** In the Evidence Tracker, change EV-004 from `Missing` to `Received`. Confirm the Executive Dashboard evidence completion changes from 80% to 90%. Record the artifact receipt date and reviewer note. Then revert if preserving the sample baseline.
2. **Risk closure:** In Findings & Actions, change F-001 from `Open` to `Closed`. Confirm the dashboard open findings drops from 3 to 2. Describe what independent evidence would justify closure.
3. **Vendor onboarding:** Examine V-004 (CareBridge Integration). Write a one-paragraph risk escalation explaining why onboarding remains blocked until the BAA issue is resolved.
4. **CISO weekly update:** Draft a 150-word update with top risks, evidence gaps, accountable owners, due dates, and requested leadership decisions.
5. **Control effectiveness:** Explain why the workbook cannot itself establish SOC 2 Type II operational effectiveness across an observation period.
